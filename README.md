# AI photo prompts

A small, practical collection of prompts for realistic AI photos. They work with most text-to-image models; we use them in [PicYep](https://picyep.com/).

## Headshots
- Professional headshot, soft window light from the left, neutral grey background, navy blazer, slight smile, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field.
- Outdoor business portrait, golden hour, blurred city street behind, natural skin texture, eye-level camera.

## Natural portraits
- Candid portrait in a cafe, warm afternoon light, looking out the window, film-like colour.
- Close-up portrait on an overcast beach, wind in the hair, muted tones.

## Pets
- Golden retriever sitting in autumn leaves, low camera angle, sharp eyes, soft background.
- Black cat on a windowsill at dusk, rim light, cosy interior.

## Holiday photos
- Family of four in matching knit sweaters by a decorated Christmas tree, warm fairy lights, natural expressions.
- Couple holding mugs on a snowy porch, evening, string lights, gentle bokeh.

## Tips
- Describe light and lens, not just the subject.
- Ask for natural skin texture to avoid a plastic look.
- Add a clear reference photo when the result should look like a specific person or pet.
